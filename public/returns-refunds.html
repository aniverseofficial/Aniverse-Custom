Returns & Refunds

Our return and refund guidance for anime figures, collectibles and merchandise.

Eligibility

Because many collectibles are fragile, returns are handled case-by-case. Contact Aniverse before sending any item back so the correct process can be provided.

Damaged deliveries

If an item arrives damaged, keep the packaging and contact Aniverse as soon as possible with clear photos or video of the package and product. This helps us assess courier or packaging damage.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where supported. Processing time can depend on the payment provider and bank.

Exclusions

Used, altered, intentionally damaged or incomplete items may not qualify for a return. Pre-order and special-order products may have additional conditions communicated before purchase.
