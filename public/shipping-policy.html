Shipping Policy

How Aniverse handles dispatch, delivery and shipping charges for orders placed in India.

Dispatch

Orders are normally packed after payment confirmation. Dispatch timing can vary by product availability, pre-order status and operational conditions.

Delivery

Delivery timelines depend on the courier network and destination. Tracking details are shared when a shipment is handed to the delivery partner.

Shipping charges

Aniverse currently offers free shipping above the threshold displayed on the storefront. Orders below that threshold may incur the standard shipping charge shown at checkout.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and reachable phone number. Contact Aniverse promptly if you notice an address error before dispatch.
