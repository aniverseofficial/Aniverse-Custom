Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of how Aniverse uses information needed to operate the store.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide during account creation, checkout and support interactions, such as name, email, phone number and delivery address.

How we use it

  • Process and deliver orders
  • Provide customer support and order updates
  • Maintain customer accounts and order history
  • Detect abuse, fraud and security issues
  • Improve the store and its services

Payments

Payment details are handled by the payment provider used at checkout. Aniverse does not need to store your full card details to process an order.

Cookies and local storage

The storefront may use browser storage for shopping-cart, wishlist and customer-session convenience. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact aniversemerchofficial@gmail.com.
