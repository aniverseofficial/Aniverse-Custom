FAQ

Answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, products and support.

Are the products original?

Aniverse aims to source authentic products and clearly identify licensed or branded products where applicable. Product details should be checked on the individual listing.

How do I track my order?

Tracking information is shared after shipment is handed to the delivery partner. You can also contact support with your order number.

Can I request a figure that is not listed?

Yes. Aniverse can take sourcing requests through its customer community and support channels.

How do I contact Aniverse?

Email aniversemerchofficial@gmail.com or use the contact page.
