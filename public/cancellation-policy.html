Cancellation Policy

How order cancellation requests are handled before dispatch.

Before dispatch

You can contact Aniverse as soon as possible to request cancellation. A cancellation is not guaranteed once an order has entered packing or shipment processing.

After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, it may need to follow the applicable return or delivery process instead of a direct cancellation.

Refund timing

If a cancellation is approved after payment, the applicable refund is initiated through the original payment method.
